Vibe Walk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Vibe Walk is a personal-project ChatGPT GPT that turns a starting point, a time window, and a vibe into a small walking itinerary. This policy explains what data the GPT and its server-side action handle, and what we do (and don't do) with it.

What we collect

When you ask Vibe Walk to plan a walk, the GPT sends three things to our server: your starting point (a Google Maps share link, an address, or a place name you provided), the time window you specified, and the mood you picked. We only see what you explicitly send to plan a walk. We don't ask for your name, email, account, location-from-GPS, contacts, calendar, or anything outside the walk request.

What we do with it

We use it to plan a walk, then we're done with it. Specifically:

  • The starting point is sent to Google Maps to resolve coordinates and find nearby stops.
  • The time window and mood shape the walk's structure server-side; they aren't sent anywhere else.
  • For operational logging (debugging and rate limiting), our server keeps short-lived records of aggregate request shape only: the mood, total minutes, neighborhood/area name, and how many stops were grounded. We do not log the raw start text you typed, your IP address beyond a short-lived rate-limit counter, or anything that identifies you personally.

Who we share it with

Vibe Walk is built on three third-party services. Each receives the minimum data needed to do its part of the job:

  • Google Maps Platform (Geocoding, Places, Routes APIs) receives your starting point and derived coordinates so we can resolve it and find walkable stops. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI / ChatGPT hosts the conversation between you and the Vibe Walk GPT. Subject to OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts the server-side action that plans the walk. Vercel maintains standard request logs (timestamp, response status, etc.) on its infrastructure. Subject to Vercel's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No tracking pixels, no analytics SDKs, no advertising.
  • No selling, sharing, or transferring your data to third parties beyond the operational ones above.
  • No persistent storage of your walk requests or any history of your walks.

Data retention

Server-side caches that help speed up repeat requests (e.g. resolved place data) live in memory and clear themselves on a short cycle. Operational logs at our hosting provider follow that provider's default retention (typically 30 days for routine logs, then deleted). We don't keep our own copies beyond what's described here.

Your choices

Stop using Vibe Walk at any time and we receive nothing further from you. If you'd like us to remove a specific log entry that you believe identifies you, or if you have any other privacy question, email us (see Contact below) and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Vibe Walk is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected in the GPT's behavior or description.
